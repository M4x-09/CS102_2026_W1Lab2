# hafizun.uz — “Signal Bridge” identity

## Concept

The custom symbol is an abstract **H** built from two stable rounded rails and one ascending connection. The rails represent dependable digital foundations; the bridge represents connected systems, collaboration, and the transformation of a business idea into a working digital product. Its upward movement communicates growth without relying on a literal stock arrow. The coral node is the moment of launch—the human creative spark and a subtle visual echo of the dot in **hafizun.uz**.

The geometry is intentionally compact, friendly, and simple enough to remain recognizable after a brief glance. It avoids shields, code brackets, devices, robots, and other generic technology-category symbols.

## Typography recommendations

- **Brand/display:** **Sora SemiBold or Bold (600–700)**. Its geometric construction complements the monogram while remaining warm and highly legible.
- **UI, websites, decks, and body copy:** **Inter Regular/Medium/SemiBold (400–600)**. It provides excellent screen clarity and a broad digital product hierarchy.
- **Fallback:** `Noto Sans, Arial, sans-serif`.

Recommended wordmark styling: lowercase, visually tight tracking, with **hafizun** dominant and **.uz** at approximately 72–76% of its size. Keep the dot coral and the suffix electric blue.

## Usage rules

- Preserve clear space around the symbol equal to at least half the width of one vertical rail.
- Do not rotate the mark, recolor individual pieces arbitrarily, add outlines, or place it on visually noisy imagery.
- Use the square primary logo for profile posts and brand announcements.
- Use the standalone icon for the Instagram avatar. The supplied composition keeps all critical geometry well inside the circular crop.
- At very small sizes, use only the icon; use the full wordmark when the available width is at least 180 px.
- Preferred backgrounds are White or Cloud. The mark may also sit on very pale blue or turquoise-tinted surfaces with sufficient contrast.

## Brand character

**Innovative · intelligent · youthful · premium · trustworthy**

The identity is designed to feel internationally relevant and service-oriented—not like a gaming team, crypto token, or generic IT vendor.
